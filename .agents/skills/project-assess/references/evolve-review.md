# Evolve toward selected goals

Use Evolve for a focused next-step plan toward the user's selected goals. It uses recorded judgment and goals; it does not prove a missing protection or assign a new score. Inspect the rationale and cited source first. Gather unknown evidence while keeping independently known gaps visible. An unconfigured target needs the user's choice. Recommend proportional goals when none are selected; do not save them automatically.

Choose one next rubric step tied to a real risk or use case. Propose the smallest useful change, observable acceptance evidence and expected upkeep against the burden ceiling. Growth can mean a simpler supported interface, not more layers. Keep existing high capability when its cost is acceptable. When capability and burden both miss their goals, rebalance: retain useful guarantees, remove avoidable upkeep and add the missing protection.

- **Testing:** protect an important public outcome or credible failure, especially authorization, durable-data integrity and costly integration regressions. Exercise the real boundary and relevant failure behavior. Avoid test quotas, coverage-percentage goals, private call-sequence assertions and speculative case matrices.
- **Extensibility:** identify a concrete variant, its authors and frequency. Try a localized function, configuration option, injected dependency or supported API before adding plugin machinery. Demonstrate the variant and preservation of existing contracts; independent or untrusted authors may need additional compatibility and isolation proof.

Evolve returns advice. During authorized implementation, make the intended change within the requested scope, preserve existing security/data/error/concurrency guarantees, and run the closest acceptance check. An intended capability addition can change behavior; explain that change rather than describing every addition as behavior-preserving cleanup. Rescan and review the resulting evidence separately from changing goals. Stop adding capability when the selected minimum and its proof are met; address remaining excess upkeep while retaining useful guarantees. Artifact counts or a passing check do not automatically increase the rating.
