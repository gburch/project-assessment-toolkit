# Community conduct

PAT welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Help
others contribute, discuss ideas respectfully, and give specific, constructive
feedback. Assume good intent while taking the impact of your words seriously.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and sharing someone's
private information without consent are not acceptable. Respect requests to stop
unwanted contact. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests and
other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when
needed. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

To request maintainer help, open an issue asking for a private follow-up without
posting sensitive details. For abuse requiring GitHub's intervention, use
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Report software vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
